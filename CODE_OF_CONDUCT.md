# Code of Conduct

ShowAI follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and languages. Discussions in English or Chinese are both welcome.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to **caopu@bupt.edu.cn**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is protected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants, depending on the severity and pattern of the behavior.
